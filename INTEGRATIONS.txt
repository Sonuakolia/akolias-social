Akolias Social Ultimate - External Integrations

The following modules are structurally prepared but require provider credentials before they can be truly live:
1. Mobile OTP: Supabase Phone provider + SMS provider (Twilio/Vonage/etc.)
2. Apple Login: Apple Developer OAuth credentials
3. Voice/Video Calls: WebRTC signaling/TURN service or provider such as LiveKit/Twilio
4. Push Notifications: FCM/APNs or web-push service
5. Live Streaming: media server/streaming provider
6. UPI/Payments: payment gateway merchant account (Razorpay/PhonePe/etc.)
7. AI Moderation: moderation API/model + server-side processing
8. Ads billing/monetization payouts: payment/compliance provider

These cannot be made operational by browser HTML alone without credentials and service accounts.
